About Us

A reputation built on
craftsmanship and care

For over a decade, we have been trusted by homeowners, interior designers and architects to supply and install flooring of the very highest standard. Every project we undertake receives our full attention — from the initial consultation through to the final fitting.

We believe the floor beneath your feet shapes the entire character of a room. That is why we never compromise on the quality of our materials, our workmanship, or our service.

What We Offer

Our flooring

Luxury Carpets

From deep-pile wool to hand-tufted natural fibres, our carpet collection spans the finest manufacturers in Britain and Europe. Underfoot comfort and enduring beauty, in every room.

Engineered Wood

Solid engineered oak, walnut and ash — crafted to perform beautifully across most environments. Timeless in appearance, built to last and fitted to the very highest standard.

Luxury Vinyl Tile

Combining the beauty of natural stone and wood with outstanding durability, our luxury vinyl tile collection offers a sophisticated finish that performs beautifully in even the most demanding environments.

Professional Installation

Our installation teams are experienced craftsmen, not contractors. Every floor is laid with precision, care and a meticulous attention to detail that ensures a result you will be proud of for years.

Bespoke Stair Runners

A beautifully fitted stair runner is one of the most impactful statements a home can make. We supply and fit bespoke runners, tailored to your staircase and your interior scheme.

Screeding

We apply self-levelling latex screed to create a perfectly flat, smooth substrate before installation. Fast-setting and highly durable, it eliminates subfloor imperfections and ensures a flawless finish underfoot.

Natural & Unnatural Sisals

From authentic natural sisal and seagrass to high-performance unnatural alternatives, our collection brings texture, warmth and character to any interior. Beautifully tactile, expertly fitted, and available in a range of weaves and colourways to suit every scheme.

In-House Fabrication

We sew bespoke binding tape directly onto carpet materials to create beautifully finished rugs and stair runners — all crafted in our own workroom. This level of in-house fabrication is rare in the industry, allowing us to maintain complete control over quality, finish and turnaround.

Wool Flatweaves

Our wool flatweave collection is a favourite choice for staircases, offering the perfect combination of durability, texture and refined style as a stair runner. Woven rather than tufted, they lie beautifully flat and wear exceptionally well underfoot. Among the brands we work with are Roger Oates and Off The Loom — two of the most respected names in the flatweave market — ensuring every runner we supply and fit meets the very highest standard.
